# Anti-pattern signal index

Signals are hypotheses. Match the number to `anti-pattern-details`.

1. **Authority gradient:** premise echoed without foundation evidence.
2. **Perfect plan:** files, APIs, and solution fixed before discovery.
3. **Parachute:** a third correction patches the same growing symptom.
4. **Lock-in:** exceptions accumulate around untouchable architecture.
5. **Fog:** abstractions lack a concrete owner or failure rule.
6. **Moving scope:** writers overlap or review watches change.
7. **Self-acceptance:** one agent defines, builds, measures, and accepts.
8. **Test-shaped proof:** checks mirror implementation or hide real failure.
9. **Edge overbuild:** large machinery protects a rare low-impact case.
10. **Loop debt:** unchanged status or failed calls repeat.
11. **Ceremony:** topology grows without decision-changing evidence.
12. **Framing capture:** alternatives inherit the proposed solution.
13. **Forked independence:** a fork is called an independent Reviewer.
14. **Attention dilution:** Q&A displaces Lead coordination and acceptance.
15. **Role pollution:** roles perform unrelated orchestration work.
16. **Status acceptance:** lifecycle or passing tests becomes the verdict.
17. **Supervisor overreach:** observation becomes implementation or acceptance.
18. **Contract-minting red test:** a new test or mock invents an unauthorized contract.
19. **Foundation ballooning:** wrappers and exceptions accumulate over a weak base.
20. **Review-loop non-convergence:** local review fixes multiply without a shared-mechanism reframe.
21. **Scout-as-Judge:** discovery evidence is promoted into a binding decision.
22. **Nested orchestration ownership:** two control planes claim the same runtime lifecycle.
